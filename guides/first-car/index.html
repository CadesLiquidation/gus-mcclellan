Car buying guide

Buying your first car.

First cars are exciting and a little nerve-wracking. Here is how to do it without the regret — and why having one person to call makes the whole thing easier.

Start with the monthly number, not the car

Figure out what you can pay each month — car payment plus insurance — before you fall in love with anything. Insurance is higher for first-time and younger drivers, so get an insurance quote on the car you are eyeing before you buy it. The right car is the one you can comfortably afford every month, not just the one you can drive off the lot today.
