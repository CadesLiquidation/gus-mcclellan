Who each one fits

A hybrid is the easy switch — no lifestyle change, just fewer trips to the gas station, and it fits almost everyone. A plug-in hybrid fits if you have a short commute and can charge at home. Fully electric fits if you can charge at home and most of your driving is local — or if you are ready for fast chargers on road trips. If you cannot charge at home, a hybrid is the better call, and Gus will say so.

Charging, honestly

Most EV owners charge at home overnight, the same way you charge your phone — you wake up to a full car. On road trips, fast chargers do the job: the IONIQ 5 can charge from 10% to 80% in about 20 minutes on a fast charger. If home charging is not an option for you, that is fine — that is exactly what the hybrids are for.

New is the way to go here

EV and hybrid tech moves fast — batteries, charging, and driver-assist tech all get better every year. Buying new gets you the latest version, the full factory warranty, and the newest safety tech. Incentives and tax credits change all the time, so ask Gus what is available right now rather than trusting something you read months ago.
