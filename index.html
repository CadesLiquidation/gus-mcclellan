How it works with me

I don’t do the shuffle where you talk to four different people and start over every time. You deal with me, start to finish.

  1. Tell me what you need. What you drive now, what you need the car for, and what you can spend. That’s enough.
  2. I’ll line up cars worth driving. New, used, Hyundai or something else. If it’s not on the lot, I’ll check what’s coming in or what I can track down.
  3. You come drive them. No pressure to decide on the spot. If it’s not the right one, we keep looking.

After the sale, you still call me. That’s the part I care about most — the best compliment is when somebody comes back, or sends me a friend.

Good questions to ask

Do I need to know which Hyundai I want?

No. Start with your routine, must-have features, budget, and how many people or how much cargo you carry. That’s enough to narrow it down.

Can Gus help with a trade-in?

Yes. Send the year, make, model, mileage, and a few honest details about condition, and he can help you understand the next step.

Can I work with Gus if I found a car on the dealership site?

Absolutely. Send the vehicle link or stock number through Facebook or text and he can help confirm the next step with that vehicle.

How does the referral bonus work?

Introduce someone who is shopping and have them mention your name. If they purchase, Gus will follow up with your $200 cash bonus and any applicable terms.

Does Gus also sell used cars?

Yes — and not just used Hyundais. Green Hyundai keeps a large pre-owned inventory alongside new Hyundais, including Hyundai Certified Used vehicles and used cars from other brands like Chevrolet, Ford, Toyota, and Jeep.

What if I want a specific used car that is not on the lot?

Tell Gus the year, make, model, and budget. He can check what’s coming in, what he can track down, or what’s close enough to be worth a look — and he’ll tell you straight if it’s not a good fit.
